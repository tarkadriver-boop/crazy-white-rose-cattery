Our Story

It all began with one very special cat named Dotty. ❤️ From the very first meeting, Dotty stole her owner's heart and sparked a lifelong love for the wonderful Devon Rex breed.

What started as a special bond soon grew into something more, leading to the creation of Crazy White Rose Cattery — a cattery built on love, passion and genuine care for these extraordinary cats.

Devon Rex cats are so much more than their unique looks. They are affectionate, intelligent, curious and incredibly people-oriented. They love being close to their humans, happily curl up for cuddles, follow their owners around the house and can even learn commands thanks to their clever and playful nature.

At Crazy White Rose Cattery, we believe every cat deserves love, attention and the very best care. For us, breeding is not simply about raising kittens; it is about giving them a safe, loving and nurturing start in life.

Our story began with Dotty. Today, we share that same love with every little Devon Rex who leaves our home, ready to bring joy, affection and a little extra magic to their new family. 🐾
