Meet Dotty

Dotty was the beginning of our love affair with the Devon Rex breed. From the moment we met her, we fell in love with her unique charm and wonderful personality.

Her affectionate nature, playful spirit and love of human company showed us just how extraordinary these cats can be. She inspired us to create Crazy White Rose Cattery and share our love for Devon Rex with other families.

Dotty will always have a very special place in our hearts. She is where our story began. ❤️
